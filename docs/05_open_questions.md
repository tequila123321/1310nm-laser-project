# 05 - Open Questions

These questions should be answered before committing to detailed simulation or a mask.

## Product

- What is the actual use case: communication, sensing, seed source, test source, or integrated photonics?
- Is the target wavelength exactly 1310 nm or a range inside the O-band?
- What output power is required after coupling?
- Is SMSR specified? If yes, what value?
- Is polarization specified?
- Is the laser uncooled or temperature-controlled?

## Device

- FP, DFB, DBR, or tunable architecture?
- InGaAsP/InP or AlGaInAs/InP active region?
- Ridge, buried heterostructure, or buried ridge?
- Direct modulation or external modulation?
- Required lifetime and qualification standard?

## Fabrication

- Which foundry or process line will fabricate the wafer?
- Is grating regrowth available?
- What lithography is available for about 205 nm DFB period?
- What minimum feature size and overlay can the process support?
- Are facet coatings available?

## Software and Data

- Which commercial licenses are already available?
- Do we have literature structures or foundry PDK examples to start from?
- What measurement equipment is available for L-I-V, spectrum, temperature, and far-field?
- Can the team run controlled pulsed measurements to separate thermal effects?
